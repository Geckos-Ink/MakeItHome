MakeItHome Web works along MakeItHome, the open source UI extension available on the App Store.

When the extension is connected, the Safari's preview shows an aurora borealis. This indicates you can activate the App Extension overscreen, accessing the rapid Safari's tabs navigation.

MakeItHome status: ConnectedNot connected

For support or issues reporting, use MakeItHome's issues/discussions page.

Basic how to use: Break the screen boundary on the Safari's preview side: if the extension is connected to MakeItHome will appears an aurora borealis.
Use mouse or trackpad scroll to move tabs previews. Click on a preview to activate a tab.
With a right click you can remove a tab from the previews list.
